About the Festival

Come Sing With Us

This December, singers of every age will gather for a weekend of music, friendship, and worship. Whether you love sacred hymns that testify of Jesus Christ or simply love to sing something fun, there is a choir waiting for you.

♬

Christ-Centered Music

A program focused on the Savior — joyful, reverent, and full of light.

🌄

Three Songs & a Finale

Each choir prepares three songs of its own, then all voices unite for one combined performance.

🤝

Everyone Welcome

Three choirs organized by age, from young children to adults. No audition required.

Three Choirs

Find Your Voice

Each choir is grouped by age so every singer can learn and perform alongside friends their own stage of life. Every choir prepares three songs of its own, then all three join together for one combined finale.

Ages 7–12

Children's Choir

Simple, heartfelt songs and plenty of energy. Young singers learn the joy of praising the Lord in song — with a fun number or two mixed in.

Ages 12–18

Youth Choir

Uplifting, engaging music for teens. A chance to build friendships, strengthen testimonies, and make something beautiful together.

Ages 18+

Adult Choir

Rich harmonies and beloved Christmas hymns centered on Jesus Christ. All skill levels welcome — bring your voice and your heart.

The Schedule

Three Days of Song

Rehearsals run Thursday and Friday evenings and Saturday morning, with a separate time slot for each choir — Children's, Youth, and Adult — leading up to the combined performance on Saturday evening.

Thursday
Dec 3

Evening Rehearsals

  • 5:30–6:45 PMChildren's Choir
  • 7:00–8:15 PMYouth Choir
  • 8:30–9:45 PMAdult Choir

Each choir meets with its director to begin learning its three songs. Come to the slot for your choir — you're free before and after it.

Friday
Dec 4

Evening Rehearsals

  • 5:30–6:45 PMChildren's Choir
  • 7:00–8:15 PMYouth Choir
  • 8:30–9:45 PMAdult Choir

More time with each choir in the same slots as Thursday, plus work on the combined finale.

Saturday
Dec 5 · AM

Morning Rehearsals

  • 10:00–10:45 AMChildren's Choir
  • 10:35–11:20 AMYouth Choir
  • 11:10–11:55 AMAdult Choir

Each 45-minute slot overlaps the next by 10 minutes, giving us time for combined-choir rehearsals before the performance.

Saturday
Dec 5 · PM

6:00 PM — Festival Performance

The concert begins at 6:00 PM. Each choir shares its three songs, closing with one combined performance celebrating the Savior. Singers should plan to arrive before 6:00 PM to gather and warm up; the audience is welcome from the time doors open. Admission is free, and all are welcome — invite family, friends, and neighbors.
