About the Festival

Come Sing With Us

This December, singers ages 7–15 will gather in one choir for a weekend of music, friendship, and worship. Whether you love sacred hymns that testify of Jesus Christ or simply love to sing something fun, come share your voice with us.

♬

Christ-Centered Music

A program focused on the Savior — joyful, reverent, and full of light.

🌄

Three Songs & a Finale

Our choir prepares three songs and a joyful Christmas finale to share with family and friends.

🤝

Every Young Voice Welcome

One choir for singers ages 7–15. All experience levels welcome, with no audition required.

The Schedule

Three Days of Song

Our choir rehearses together for 90 minutes Thursday and Friday evenings and Saturday afternoon, immediately before the 6:00 PM festival performance.

Thursday
Dec 3

Evening Rehearsal

  • 5:30–7:00 PMFestival Choir · Ages 7–15

Meet your director and fellow singers as we begin learning our three songs and Christmas finale together.

Friday
Dec 4

Evening Rehearsal

  • 5:30–7:00 PMFestival Choir · Ages 7–15

Continue practicing our songs and finale, growing in confidence and bringing the music to life.

Saturday
Dec 5 · PM

Afternoon Rehearsal

  • 4:30–6:00 PMFestival Choir · Ages 7–15

One final rehearsal together to polish our songs and warm up, leading directly into the 6:00 PM performance. Singers should arrive ready to rehearse at 4:30 PM.

Saturday
Dec 5 · PM

6:00 PM — Festival Performance

The concert begins at 6:00 PM, following the choir's 4:30–6:00 PM rehearsal. Our choir shares its three songs and a joyful Christmas finale celebrating the Savior. The audience is welcome from the time doors open. Admission is free, and all are welcome — invite family, friends, and neighbors.
